Legacy of Trusted & Affordable Healthcare

Sri Ramakrishna Hospital was originally founded in 1972 by Dr. N. Ramakrishna Reddy as a humble clinic in Allagadda. Due to exceptional patient care and deep trust among the public, it steadily grew into a renowned medical hub. In 2002, specialized Obstetrics & Gynecology services were introduced by Dr. N. Sujatha. Over the decades, under the visionary leadership of Dr. N. L. Lakshmi Sekhar Reddy and Dr. N. Sujatha, the institution expanded with advanced infrastructure, including 25 beds, air-conditioned rooms, a modern operation theatre, and high-tech laboratory systems.
